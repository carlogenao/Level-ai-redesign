Privacy, in plain language.

About this preview. This page is a redesign preview. Inquiries sent from this preview are saved in a private store attached to the preview, readable only by Level AI’s owner account. They are not sent to the live site’s database, and no email or text notification is sent. Lily chat is not connected here.

This site introduces Level AI’s services and provides direct email and phone links, plus optional AI website chat.

Contact and saved requests

The contact links open your email or phone app. The inquiry form saves your name, email, phone, company and optional website and request details for Level AI’s owner to review. It does not send an email or SMS notification automatically. Information you choose to email or discuss by phone is received by Level AI through those services. Records persist until manually removed. Contact info@level-ai.io about access or deletion.

Lily website chat and phone links

On the live site, opening Text Lily loads the ElevenLabs text widget and connects your browser to ElevenLabs. Sending a message shares your text and conversation data with ElevenLabs. Lily is AI. Website chat conversations are stored. Text Lily does not need your microphone. Call Lily opens your device’s phone app with the listed number. Do not share sensitive or confidential information.

Discovery scheduling

Opening the discovery calendar connects to Motion. Booking details you enter are processed by Motion and shared with Level AI to arrange your meeting. Review the calendar’s time zone before confirming. Booking does not opt you into marketing texts.

Text messages and consent

Text Lily is website chat, not SMS. This site does not enroll visitors in a text-messaging program or send automated SMS. Entering a phone number in the inquiry form does not provide consent to marketing text messages.

Production is scoped separately

Collection, consent, retention, security and integration requirements for client projects are scoped separately. This site does not establish those commitments.
